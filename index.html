Guidelines

Ticket Verification

Please ensure your digital or physical ticket QR code is clear and visible for scanning at the stadium gates. Do not share your QR code online.

ID Proof Requirement

You must carry a valid government-issued ID (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN, or Passport) matching the name on your ticket for verification.

Prohibited Items

Large bags, outside food and beverages, laptops, laser pointers, and hazardous materials are strictly prohibited inside the stadium premises.
